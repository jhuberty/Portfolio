Agricultural Equipment & Field Work

Project Overview

Hands-on agricultural equipment and field operations experience with focus on machinery use, maintenance support, and troubleshooting.

My Role

As an Agricultural Operations Technician, I supported daily field operations across approximately 8 acres of crop production while operating and maintaining tractors, implements, irrigation equipment, and other farm machinery.

Equipment & Field Operations

Operated tractors and farm implements to support soil preparation, irrigation, fertilization, harvesting, and general field operations. Worked with drip irrigation, traveling big-gun sprinklers, and hand-move sprinkler systems across a variety of crops.

Manufacturing & Fabrication

Fabricated and repaired agricultural-equipment components to support field operations, including repairing a lister bar. Diagnosed mechanical issues, selected practical repair approaches, and completed small fabrication and maintenance tasks to return equipment to safe, usable condition.

Testing & Troubleshooting

Monitored equipment performance and irrigation operation during field work, checking for issues such as leaks, improper setup, mechanical wear, or inconsistent operation. Adjusted equipment setup and communicated issues to support timely repairs and reliable field performance.

Final Result

Supported efficient crop-production operations by helping maintain functional equipment and irrigation systems while completing field tasks safely and on schedule.

What I Learned

Developed a stronger understanding of how agricultural equipment, irrigation, soil conditions, and field operations interact in real production environments. This experience strengthened my equipment-operation, maintenance, troubleshooting, safety, and agricultural-engineering skills.
