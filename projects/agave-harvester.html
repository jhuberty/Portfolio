Agave Harvester Senior Project

Project Overview

This ongoing senior project is developing an agave harvester that uses a modified grape harvester to remove approximately 80% of an agave plant's leaves. The team is building on the previous senior project's work while evaluating whether the cutting concept can operate reliably on an older machine with unexpected mechanical issues.

My Role

Senior Project Manager

I lead project planning and team coordination while helping guide the next iteration of the cutting system. The project has strengthened my leadership and SolidWorks skills as I work with the team to turn prior project work into a testable design.

Design

The current concept uses a cutter mounted to a steel plate. Early testing showed that the plate bent while cutting agave leaves, so the team is redesigning the blade structure to be stronger before testing the concept again. If the stronger structure works, the team will refine the design; otherwise, it will return to concept development and evaluate other approaches.

Manufacturing & Fabrication

The team has fabricated and tested an initial steel cutting plate with an attached cutter. The plate bending under load identified a structural issue that must be addressed before further development, so the next build will focus on a stronger blade structure for another test iteration.

Testing & Troubleshooting

During the initial cutting test, the steel plate bent while attempting to cut leaves. The team is using that result to guide the next design iteration, then will test the stronger structure and repeat the process as needed. Because the machine is older and has many unexpected issues, troubleshooting and repeated testing are central to evaluating the concept.

Final Result

The project is ongoing. The first test established that the original cutting-plate structure was not strong enough, and the next step is to test a reinforced blade structure. Results from that test will determine whether the team improves the current concept or returns to the design board.

What I Learned

This project is developing my leadership, SolidWorks, problem-solving, testing, and troubleshooting skills. The early test reinforced the importance of learning from failure, strengthening a design based on observed results, and continuing to validate ideas through repeated testing.
